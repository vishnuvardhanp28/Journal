# USC-SIPI research image benchmark

This curated subset contains 12 grayscale and 10 RGB TIFF files: 10 original monochrome images, 10 original RGB images, and two grayscale transforms of the Mandrill/Baboon and Peppers images to enable paired modality checks on familiar benchmark content. The source RGB files are also retained in the color subset.

The official database describes the images as for research purposes but states that it does not hold many copyrights and that rights are unknown for many files. Per-image rights notes, source IDs, transformations, dimensions, and SHA-256 hashes are in `manifest.csv` and `manifest.json`. The catalog identifies Jelly beans images (4.1.07, 4.1.08) as USC photographs free for research; other selected originals have unknown or uncertain rights. Keep these for research analysis; before reproducing any original or transformed image in a submitted paper, resolve reuse rights with the journal/publisher and copyright holder.
